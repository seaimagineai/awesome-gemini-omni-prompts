## 01｜末班站台的重逢

<!-- catalog:copy:start -->
[复制全文 / Download TXT](copy/cinematic-storytelling-01.txt) · [本页索引 / Case index](#case-index)
<!-- catalog:copy:end -->

**模式：** 文生视频 · **画幅：** 16:9 · **重点：** 双人表演、中文对白、雨声层次

```text
Create a restrained 10-second cinematic drama with native audio.

Scene: the final platform of a small mountain railway station at night. Cold rain falls beyond the canopy; a single warm vending-machine light reflects on wet concrete. No other passengers.
Characters: a woman in a charcoal wool coat holding a repaired red umbrella; a man in a faded navy work jacket carrying a small overnight bag. Both are in their early thirties. Keep their faces, clothes and relative height consistent.

[0-3s] Wide locked shot. The man steps off the last train as its doors close behind him. He notices the red umbrella. Only rain, train brakes and a quiet electrical hum.
[3-7s] Slow lateral push into a two-shot. The woman raises her eyes but does not approach. He exhales and grips the bag strap once.
[7-10s] Close two-shot without a cut. She says softly in Mandarin Chinese: "伞修好了。" After a one-second pause, he answers: "我也是。" The train departs and warm window reflections travel across their faces.

Performance: micro-expressions, incomplete smiles, no melodramatic crying.
Camera: 50mm lens, shallow but believable focus, subtle dolly, natural motion blur.
Audio: precise rain perspective, receding train, fabric movement; no music and no subtitles.
Do not include: extra people, station names, logos, text, watermark, face changes, lip movement before dialogue.
```

**改造方向：** 把两句对白替换成任意语言；只改变台词，不要同时更换地点和镜头。

## 02｜屋顶快递：一镜到底追逐

<!-- catalog:copy:start -->
[复制全文 / Download TXT](copy/cinematic-storytelling-02.txt) · [本页索引 / Case index](#case-index)
<!-- catalog:copy:end -->

**模式：** 文生视频 · **画幅：** 9:16 · **重点：** 连续镜头、物理运动、竖屏动作

```text
Format: 9:16 vertical, 10 seconds. One continuous unbroken shot, no cuts.

At blue hour in a dense hillside town, an agile bicycle courier wearing a mustard jacket races on foot across connected tiled rooftops while protecting a small paper parcel. A flock of pigeons becomes the moving foreground, not an obstacle.

Begin half a meter behind the courier at shoulder height. The camera follows as they vault one low gap, duck under a clothesline, slide two meters down wet tiles, catch a copper gutter, then swing onto a flat roof. Every landing transfers weight naturally; tiles shift only where touched. At 7s the parcel almost slips, the courier catches it with one hand, and the camera arcs to the front for the final breathless look.

Audio: shoes on ceramic tile, jacket fabric, close breathing, pigeons taking flight, distant evening market. A tight original percussion pulse follows the running cadence but never covers the effects. No dialogue.
Lighting: cool sky, warm window squares, wet reflections, realistic exposure changes.
Keep the courier, parcel and jacket unchanged. No teleporting, no impossible jumps, no drone view, no slow motion, no text, no logos, no watermark.
```

## 03｜瓷窑开炉：历史工艺瞬间

<!-- catalog:copy:start -->
[复制全文 / Download TXT](copy/cinematic-storytelling-03.txt) · [本页索引 / Case index](#case-index)
<!-- catalog:copy:end -->

**模式：** 文生视频 · **画幅：** 16:9 · **重点：** 世界知识、历史质感、群体反应

```text
Create a historically grounded 10-second scene in a riverside porcelain workshop in southern China during the early eighteenth century. Treat it as observational historical cinema, not fantasy.

[0-4s] A kiln master and three apprentices pull open the brick kiln mouth with long iron tools. Orange heat blooms into the cool predawn workshop; fine ash drifts upward. Camera at waist height makes a slow, cautious push forward.
[4-7s] Using period-appropriate wooden racks and padded grips, they reveal a row of blue-and-white bowls. One apprentice shields their face from the heat; another checks a glaze surface without touching it.
[7-10s] The master rotates one bowl toward window light and gives a small satisfied nod. End on the crackle pattern becoming visible as the bowl cools.

Look: natural linen, soot, worn timber, brick dust, restrained color, no modern factory cleanliness.
Audio: kiln roar, brick scrape, tool ring, cloth movement, quiet dawn birds. No score, no narration, no dialogue.
Accuracy constraints: plausible tools, clothing, kiln behavior and handling; no modern objects, no printed labels, no palace setting, no magical glow, no text or watermark.
```

## 04｜门下的第二道影子

<!-- catalog:copy:start -->
[复制全文 / Download TXT](copy/cinematic-storytelling-04.txt) · [本页索引 / Case index](#case-index)
<!-- catalog:copy:end -->

**模式：** 文生视频 · **画幅：** 16:9 · **重点：** 心理惊悚、有限揭示、声音反转

```text
Create a 10-second psychological suspense scene with no gore and no monster reveal.

Scene: a night security desk in an empty municipal archive. Fluorescent lights turn off in rows toward a closed records-room door. An analog desk fan continues turning.
[0-3s] Fixed medium shot from behind the guard. A knock sounds once from the closed door. The guard stops writing. Under the door, one human shadow is visible.
[3-6s] The guard slowly stands and takes one step closer. The first shadow remains still while a second, much narrower shadow crosses behind it from left to right. The fan squeak stops abruptly.
[6-9s] Very slow push toward the gap beneath the door. The guard whispers in English, "The building is closed." From the other side, the same voice answers at exactly the same volume: "I know."
[9-10s] The desk phone rings behind camera. Hard cut to black on the first ring.

Lighting: sickly practical fluorescence, deep but readable shadows, no colored horror lighting.
Audio must carry the tension: room tone, paper, fan motor, two distinct spatial voice positions, one phone ring. No music.
Do not show a creature, face, blood, subtitles, logos, extra doors or watermark.
```

## 05｜夏到冬：首尾帧季节插值

**模式：** 首帧 + 尾帧 · **画幅：** 与输入一致 · **重点：** 连续转场

**输入：** Image1 为盛夏果园，同机位 Image2 为冬季雪后果园。

```text
[# Sources <FIRST_FRAME>@Image1 <LAST_FRAME>@Image2]
Create a smooth 10-second seasonal transition from Image1 to Image2. Use the images as the exact first and final frames.

The camera advances slowly along the same central path without changing lens height. The season changes through continuous physical cues rather than a dissolve: green leaves yellow and detach in the wind; fallen leaves collect, become rimed with frost, and receive the first snow; warm afternoon sunlight lowers into cold blue twilight. Tree trunks, path geometry and fence posts stay locked to their original positions. A small wooden basket remains beside the third tree throughout and gradually gathers snow.

Audio evolves continuously from summer insects and leaves to autumn wind and finally the soft absorption of snowfall. One restrained musical drone changes from warm strings to glass harmonics. No dialogue.
No jump cut, no camera teleport, no new buildings, no people, no text, no logo, no watermark.
```

## 06｜倒放的线索：黑色电影谜案

<!-- catalog:copy:start -->
[复制全文 / Download TXT](copy/cinematic-storytelling-06.txt) · [本页索引 / Case index](#case-index)
<!-- catalog:copy:end -->

**模式：** 文生视频 · **画幅：** 16:9 · **重点：** 倒序动作、可读文字

```text
Create a 10-second monochrome neo-noir clue reveal in a single hotel corridor.

[0-3s] Start on a brass room key lying in spilled water. Water droplets move backward into a fallen glass while the camera tracks low beside them. Footsteps are heard in reverse, subtle and unsettling.
[3-7s] The key slides backward across carpet into a gloved hand. The camera rises with the hand, revealing only the lower half of a cream trench coat in a mirror. The corridor clock hands rotate backward.
[7-10s] Normal time resumes with a clean audio snap. The gloved person turns the key tag toward camera. Exact readable engraved text: "ROOM 307". Hold it readable for two seconds while an elevator bell rings off screen.

Look: high-contrast black-and-white film, wet highlights, fine grain, controlled halation.
No dialogue, no face reveal, no extra text, no misspelled key tag, no supernatural body distortion, no logos, no watermark.
```

## 07｜电梯里的沉默喜剧

<!-- catalog:copy:start -->
[复制全文 / Download TXT](copy/cinematic-storytelling-07.txt) · [本页索引 / Case index](#case-index)
<!-- catalog:copy:end -->

**模式：** 文生视频 · **画幅：** 9:16 · **重点：** 固定机位、表演节奏、无对白

```text
Format: 9:16, 10 seconds, fixed security-camera-like framing from the upper front corner of a small office elevator. One shot, no cuts.

Three coworkers stand in formal clothes, each secretly carrying the same tiny cactus as a farewell gift. At 2s the elevator jolts gently and all three reveal their plants at once to steady them. They notice the coincidence. At 5s the doors open to reveal a fourth coworker outside holding an enormous cactus in a wheeled pot. Everyone silently looks at the tiny plants, then the enormous one. At 9s the fourth coworker slowly backs away as the doors close.

Performance: restrained deadpan timing, small eye movements, natural balance correction; nobody looks into the camera.
Audio: elevator motor, soft jolt, clothing rustle, one polite arrival chime, pot wheel squeak. No music, no dialogue, no laugh track.
Preserve all four people and plant sizes; no duplicate limbs, no text, no logos, no watermark.
```

## 08｜零重力温室告警

<!-- catalog:copy:start -->
[复制全文 / Download TXT](copy/cinematic-storytelling-08.txt) · [本页索引 / Case index](#case-index)
<!-- catalog:copy:end -->

**模式：** 文生视频 · **画幅：** 16:9 · **重点：** 科幻物理、节奏升级、原生声音

```text
Create a grounded 10-second science-fiction emergency inside a rotating orbital greenhouse. The design is functional and near-future, not military.

[0-3s] Wide interior view along the curved greenhouse aisle. Artificial gravity weakens: loose soil grains and irrigation droplets begin lifting from planters. A botanist in a pale utility suit clips one boot to a floor rail.
[3-7s] The camera performs a slow roll matching the station rotation. A transparent water sphere grows around a seedling; the botanist pushes off once, catches the plant tray and anchors it. Motion follows inertia with no sudden direction changes.
[7-10s] Red emergency light shifts to steady green as gravity returns gradually. Droplets fall back into the tray. The botanist says in Spanish, out of breath: "Sigue viva." A soft system tone confirms stabilization.

Audio: ventilation, alarm pulses, floating objects tapping surfaces, one short Spanish line. No music, no subtitles.
Keep suit, face, tray and greenhouse layout consistent. No flames, weapons, aliens, exterior cutaway, logos, other text or watermark.
```

## 组合建议

- **角色一致性：** 为主角提供一张正面角色图和一张服装/道具图，分别声明 `<IMAGE_REF_0>`、`<IMAGE_REF_1>`，不要让同一张图同时充当首帧和风格参考。
- **对白不稳定：** 减少同一镜头的人物数量，把台词压缩到一句，并写明“spoken once / do not repeat / no subtitles”。
- **动作像幻灯片：** 改成“主体动作 + 相机运动 + 环境反应”，并明确 `continuous physical motion, not a slideshow`。
- **故事装不下：** 把转折点放在第一个 10 秒的末尾，再用 `previous_interaction_id` 续写下一场。
