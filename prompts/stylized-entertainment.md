## 02｜霓虹鱼市的包裹追逐

**模式：** 文生动画 · **画幅：** 16:9 · **重点：** 动作连贯、原创动画方向

```text
Create a 10-second original animated chase in a floating night market built from boats. Hand-painted 2D backgrounds, graphic shadow shapes, visible dry-brush accents, fluid character animation on twos with faster effects on ones.

A teenage messenger with a teal rain cape protects a glowing square parcel while crossing three linked boats. Begin with a low side-tracking shot. The messenger leaps over a fish crate, uses a hanging lantern rope to swing across a widening gap, and lands in a produce boat. A small mechanical gull pursues from above, clipping lantern strings but never touching people. At 8s the parcel's glow switches off; the gull overshoots into fog, and the messenger smiles briefly.

Audio: wooden hull knocks, rope strain, market voices as indistinct texture, hand percussion accelerating with footfalls, one comic metal flutter when the gull misses. No dialogue.
Keep cape, parcel, gull scale and boat layout consistent. No slideshow, no still-image pans, no recognizable franchise design, no text, logo or watermark.
```

**模式：** 文生定格动画 · **画幅：** 4:3 构图输出于 16:9 · **重点：** 材料感、喜剧节拍

```text
Create a 10-second handcrafted clay stop-motion comedy, framed as a 4:3 miniature kitchen within a 16:9 canvas.

A thumb-sized clay cook in a tall paper hat flips one blueberry pancake. At 2s the pancake sticks to the low ceiling. The cook looks up, waits, places the plate carefully beneath it, and counts silently with three finger taps. At 7s the pancake drops, bounces once on the plate, and lands perfectly. One blueberry rolls off; the cook catches it in the hat at 9s.

Animation: intentional stop-motion cadence, tiny fingerprint texture, slight practical set vibration, consistent scale. The character uses two hands only.
```

**模式：** 文生喜剧短视频 · **画幅：** 9:16 · **重点：** Meme 节奏、可读字幕卡而非挪用模板

```text
Create an original 9:16 workplace comedy, 10 seconds, with no reference to existing meme templates.

[0-3s] A serious office worker types rapidly in a quiet open-plan office. Exact small clock text in the corner: "4:59 PM". Camera slowly pushes toward the monitor, but its contents remain abstract and unreadable.
[3-6s] The clock changes exactly to "5:00 PM". Every desk lamp switches from white to warm amber in sequence. The worker removes one formal jacket sleeve; underneath is a glittery dance sleeve.
[6-10s] Without changing expression, the worker rolls away in the chair while two potted plants rotate like tiny stage lights. Exact final text: "WEEKEND MODE". Hold readable.

Audio: keyboard rhythm transforms into an original disco beat at 5s, chair wheels, one restrained office printer chirp. No dialogue, no laugh track.
Keep the same worker and office; only the quoted text may appear. No brand UI, no copyrighted song, no extra limbs, no logo or watermark.
```

**模式：** 文生音乐现场 · **画幅：** 16:9 · **重点：** 原创音乐、乐器同步、城市环境声

```text
Create a 10-second live rooftop jazz performance at sunset with an original composition. Trio: upright bass, brushed snare, muted trumpet. One continuous semicircular camera move, no cuts.

Begin close to the bass player's left hand performing a plausible walking line. Arc past the drummer, whose brushes match the audible rhythm, then settle on the trumpeter as they play one short four-note motif. At 7s a gust lifts sheet-music corners; the drummer catches the page edge with one brush hand without breaking pulse. End on the three musicians exchanging a small smile while the final muted note decays.

Natural sunset exposure, documentary lensing, same musicians and instruments throughout. No impossible fingering, duplicate instruments, text, logos or watermark.
```

## 风格提示的安全写法

用“媒介 + 工艺 + 线条/帧感 + 色彩 + 光线 + 材料”描述你想要的视觉。例如：`hand-painted 2D backgrounds, dry-brush shadows, animation on twos, indigo and amber palette`。不要用“模仿某位在世艺术家”替代真正的美术设计。
