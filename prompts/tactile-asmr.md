```text
Use Image1 as the exact opening of a 10-second, 16:9 macro material study. A transparent amber glass pear stands upright on charcoal slate. An obsidian blade enters from the upper right and just touches the unbroken surface. Several small star-shaped inclusions are visible within the core. Treat the pear as a deliberately imaginary, sliceable glass material; it is not real fruit or a real cutting demonstration.

[0-3s] Hold the camera steady at the original low three-quarter angle. The blade presses downward through the near-right side at a slow, even speed. A fine luminous seam follows the cutting edge. The pear stays anchored by its own weight; its stem and silhouette remain unchanged. Keep the blade rigid and show its edge penetrating the material rather than passing through invisibly.
[3-7s] Complete one continuous cut that separates a single thin outer slice. The slice leans away and settles on the slate at the right, exposing a flat translucent cross-section. The existing star-shaped inclusions remain embedded in the larger standing piece, with their arrangement unchanged and clearer through the cut face. Preserve the removed slice's curved outer skin and matching flat inner face.
[7-10s] Withdraw the blade upward along its entry path. Rack focus gently from the resting slice to the largest central star-shaped inclusion. Amber caustics brighten beneath the cut face and settle. End with exactly two pear pieces, one whole stem and an unobstructed view of the central inclusion.

Audio: close dry glass-like ticks during penetration, one delicate chime as the slice meets slate, then quiet room tone. No music, chewing, wet flesh sounds or exaggerated shattering. No hands, blood, sparks, loose shards, extra cuts, regenerated pear sections, camera orbit or text.
```

## 02｜月白陶壳：一条裂缝露出蓝色晶洞

<!-- catalog:copy:start -->
[复制全文 / Download TXT](copy/tactile-asmr-02.txt) · [本页索引 / Case index](#case-index)
<!-- catalog:copy:end -->

```text
Create a 10-second, 16:9 locked macro shot of a palm-sized, matte-white ceramic shell resting in a shallow black sand tray. A narrow blue crystalline cavity is concealed inside. One brass stylus touches the shell at its top; show only the tool, with no hand in frame.

[0-3s] The stylus presses once. A single hairline fracture travels slowly from the contact point toward the near edge, accompanied by a dry ceramic tick.
[3-7s] The two sides open a few millimetres under that pressure, revealing a fixed cluster of tiny cobalt crystals. Three small ceramic crumbs fall into the sand and remain there. Keep the shell's outer contour and thickness consistent; do not grow new crystals.
[7-10s] Lift the stylus away. Hold focus on the exposed cavity while a soft side light reveals its depth. The shell stays in two connected halves.

Audio: one close tap, quiet brittle crackles and three soft sand impacts. No music, explosions or wet sounds. No cuts, glitter clouds, morphing, extra tools, text or logos. This is an imagined material study, not a repair or safety demonstration.
```

<!-- catalog:copy:end -->

```text
Create a 10-second, 16:9 top-down macro shot of fine terracotta kinetic sand, evenly levelled in a shallow square tray. One small brass roller with a repeating chevron pattern rests against the upper edge. The camera and tray remain still.

[0-3s] Roll the tool slowly downward into the sand. Its embossed surface leaves a continuous fishbone track directly behind the contact line. Keep the sand undisturbed everywhere else.
[3-7s] Continue at constant speed through the centre. Tiny grains slump along both raised edges, while each repeated chevron stays crisp and evenly spaced. The cylinder must visibly rotate rather than slide across the surface.
[7-10s] Stop before reaching the lower edge and lift the roller vertically away. Hold on the complete uninterrupted track, with loose grains settling naturally into the shallow channels.

Audio: intimate dry granular crunch, faint brass axle friction and a final soft release. No music or voice. Preserve the initial sand volume and tray boundaries. No reverse playback, magically repaired sand, duplicated roller, additional patterns, hands, text or camera movement.
```

<!-- catalog:copy:end -->

```text
Create a 10-second, 16:9 macro material experiment on a black studio surface. A clear rectangular ice block contains a vertical chain of six trapped air bubbles. One warm copper sphere rests in a shallow depression on its upper face. Treat this as an accelerated artistic visualization, not measured physical footage.

[0-3s] A thin reflective meltwater ring forms around the sphere. Slowly push the camera closer without changing the viewing angle. Keep the ice block edges stable.
[3-7s] The sphere descends several millimetres as a smooth cylindrical cavity forms beneath it. The topmost trapped bubble reaches the cavity and escapes as one small surface bubble. Keep the other five bubbles in their original positions.
[7-10s] The sphere stops descending. A single water bead runs down the block's near face and reaches the table. End with the cavity, sphere and remaining bubbles clearly visible.

Audio: faint ice ticks, one small bubble pop and a water bead landing. No sizzling fire sound, steam cloud or music. No fragmentation, new spheres, hollow transparent copper, text or sudden cuts.
```

```text
Create a 10-second, 16:9 fixed macro shot of a rectangular block of blue-and-white marbled wax on a pale wooden bench. A small steel plane rests against the near upper corner, oriented to travel away from the camera. Show the tool only, with no visible hand.

[0-3s] Push the plane slowly forward. Its edge shaves a single thin ribbon, which curls upward through the tool opening. The new flat face exposes a continuous blue-and-white pattern beneath.

[7-10s] Finish the stroke at the far edge. Stop the tool and let the final curl settle on the bench. Hold focus on the clean new face and the complete ribbon beside it.
```

```text
Create a 10-second, 16:9 macro shot of a pearlescent elastic membrane stretched taut across one circular metal hoop. A single clear water bead rests slightly left of centre. The hoop lies almost horizontally against a dark background, with a narrow white strip light reflected in the bead.

[0-3s] Tilt the hoop a few degrees to the right using an unseen support. The bead begins to roll, depressing the membrane beneath it. The membrane stretches smoothly without tearing.
[3-7s] Follow the bead with a very small camera pan. Its reflection changes continuously as it crosses the centre. A shallow travelling wrinkle forms behind it and relaxes. Preserve exactly one bead and one continuous membrane.
[7-10s] Return the hoop to level before the bead reaches the edge. It slows, oscillates once and settles into a rounder shape. End with its reflection sharply resolved.

Audio: a faint rubber tension creak and delicate water contact sounds against otherwise quiet room tone. No popping, speech or music. No rainbow explosions, fluid duplication, punctures, hands, text or abrupt speed changes.
```

## 08｜磁珠编织：一枚环从平面竖起来

<!-- catalog:copy:start -->
[复制全文 / Download TXT](copy/tactile-asmr-08.txt) · [本页索引 / Case index](#case-index)
<!-- catalog:copy:end -->

```text
Create a 10-second, 16:9 locked macro tabletop animation of twenty identical polished steel beads arranged in a closed circle on matte navy felt. A small cylindrical magnet is visible above the topmost bead. This is a deliberately choreographed material animation, not a scientific demonstration of magnetic behaviour.

[0-3s] Lower the magnet until it contacts the top bead. Raise it slowly, lifting that bead while its two neighbours follow. Keep every adjacent pair connected.
[3-7s] Continue lifting until the bead circle stands upright on its lowest bead, becoming a vertical ring. Each bead rotates naturally at its contact points; preserve the count and circular order. Felt fibres compress beneath the bottom bead.
[7-10s] Stop the magnet and let the ring sway once, then settle. A narrow light strip travels across the polished surfaces as focus remains on the front half of the ring.

Audio: restrained individual metallic clicks and a soft felt drag. No music, machine roar or voice. No extra beads, elastic stretching, disappearing supports, text or camera orbit.
```
