## 01｜蓝色旅行箱：云海天文台

[![参考首帧 / Reference Image1](../assets/showcase-v2-06.png)](../assets/showcase-v2-06.png)
<!-- catalog:copy:end -->

<!-- source: data/showcase-v2.json#suitcase-observatory; keep prompt synchronized -->

```text
Animate Image1 into a continuous 10-second, 16:9 miniature-diorama film. Inside an open blue hard-shell suitcase, a rocky mountain supports a brass-domed observatory above a shallow sea of clouds. One tiny steam locomotive with two carriages rests on a circular track around the mountain. A copper control knob sits on the front-right exterior panel. No hand is visible in the opening frame.

[0-2s] One adult hand enters from the near frame edge, reaches the copper knob on the front-right exterior panel, turns it one quarter-turn clockwise and releases it. The hand withdraws through the nearest frame edge without entering the miniature landscape. The suitcase, hinges, mountain and rails remain stationary. A tiny warm lamp beside the observatory door comes on.
[2-6s] The train starts gently and travels clockwise along the existing rails. Its three vehicles stay coupled, with wheels contacting the track. A narrow puff of steam rises from the locomotive and disperses above the carriage roofs. Slowly push the camera toward the mountain while keeping both suitcase rims visible to preserve the scale contrast.
[6-10s] The observatory dome rotates a small distance and its slit opens to reveal a short telescope aimed at one fixed star in the existing night sky. Clouds drift around the mountain base without spilling over the suitcase rim. Let the train continue behind the mountain, partially occluded rather than disappearing. Stop the camera with the glowing observatory above the cloud line.

Audio: close copper-knob click, miniature rail rhythm, soft gear movement and a restrained airy room ambience. No full-size train horn, narration or music. Preserve scale and object counts. No extra hands, flying rails, expanding suitcase, text, logos, time-lapse sky or cuts.
```

## 02｜茶杯潮汐：灯塔岛迎来一艘小船

<!-- catalog:copy:start -->
[复制全文 / Download TXT](copy/miniature-worlds-02.txt) · [本页索引 / Case index](#case-index)
<!-- catalog:copy:end -->

```text
Create a 10-second, 16:9 miniature film of a tiny rocky lighthouse island inside a wide white ceramic teacup. A narrow ring of water surrounds the island; one red toy-sized sailboat floats near the front. Keep the cup handle visible at right and a teaspoon outside it to establish scale.

[0-3s] Begin a slow lateral camera slide. A gentle ripple lifts the sailboat once without spilling water over the cup rim. The lighthouse lens begins a measured clockwise rotation.
[3-7s] The sailboat travels left around the island, following the water channel. Its sail flexes subtly; its hull stays at the surface. The rotating beam passes across the rocky shore and briefly reaches the cup's inner wall.
[7-10s] The boat reaches a small jetty on the left and slows beside it. End with the lighthouse, boat and entire cup rim still visible. Keep all structures fixed to the island.

Audio: delicate lapping water, a tiny rope creak and quiet room tone. No seagull swarm, ocean-sized waves, narration or music. No full-size humans, new boats, changing cup shape, letters or cuts.
```

## 03｜书脊峡谷：维修小车穿过装订线桥

<!-- catalog:copy:start -->
[复制全文 / Download TXT](copy/miniature-worlds-03.txt) · [本页索引 / Case index](#case-index)
<!-- catalog:copy:end -->

```text
Create a 10-second, 16:9 miniature diorama shot across the open spine of an old unprinted clothbound book. Raised paper layers form two canyon walls. A small maintenance trolley sits on a narrow wooden bridge suspended from visible binding threads, spanning the gutter. No writing appears on any page.

[0-3s] Slowly lower the camera toward bridge height while keeping the book's outer edges visible. The trolley's small lamp switches on. One thread support vibrates slightly as the wheels start moving.
[3-7s] The trolley crosses from left to right at walking pace. The bridge sags gently beneath its weight and recovers behind it. The wheels remain on the two rails; its lamp illuminates the paper strata ahead.
[7-10s] The trolley enters a shallow tunnel cut into the right paper wall. Its rear lamp remains visible for a moment, then disappears behind the tunnel edge. Stop the camera on the now-empty bridge.

Audio: miniature wheel clicks, soft thread tension and paper rustle. No dialogue, music, page-turning or earthquake. No floating bridge, human hands, printed text, morphing book or cuts.
```

## 04｜玻璃罐温室：微型机器人为番茄授粉

<!-- catalog:copy:start -->
[复制全文 / Download TXT](copy/miniature-worlds-04.txt) · [本页索引 / Case index](#case-index)
<!-- catalog:copy:end -->

```text
Create a 10-second, 16:9 macro miniature greenhouse film inside a clear cylindrical glass jar. One dwarf tomato vine climbs a thin brass trellis. A tiny wheeled gardening robot with one soft brush arm stands beside a single yellow flower. Keep a full-size pencil outside the jar for scale.

[0-3s] Push the camera slowly closer through the clear front glass, retaining subtle curved reflections. The robot advances along a visible soil path and stops beneath the flower. Its wheels leave two shallow tracks.
[3-7s] The brush arm rises and touches the flower gently twice. The stem bends slightly and springs back. Show no instant fruit growth or exaggerated pollen cloud. Existing green tomatoes elsewhere on the vine remain unchanged.
[7-10s] The robot lowers its arm and reverses along its own tracks. A condensation bead on the jar wall slides a short distance, refracting the trellis behind it. End on the intact flower.

Audio: a tiny motor whirr, two soft brush contacts and quiet greenhouse ambience. No speech or music. No new robots, insects, text, broken glass, giant plant growth or cuts.
```

## 05｜胡桃壳港口：吊机卸下一袋微型咖啡豆

<!-- catalog:copy:start -->
[复制全文 / Download TXT](copy/miniature-worlds-05.txt) · [本页索引 / Case index](#case-index)
<!-- catalog:copy:end -->

```text
Create a 10-second, 16:9 miniature port inside one half of a walnut shell resting on a linen tablecloth. A shallow resin-like harbour holds one tiny wooden cargo boat. A small brass crane is fixed to a dock along the shell's inner edge. Its hook is already attached to one burlap sack on the boat.

[0-3s] Hold a low three-quarter view showing the walnut rim. The crane winds up its visible cable, lifting the sack just clear of the boat. The boat rocks slightly and returns toward level.
[3-7s] Rotate the crane arm slowly toward the dock. Keep the sack hanging beneath the hook with a small lagging swing. The crane base remains firmly attached to the dock; the cable stays taut.
[7-10s] Lower the sack onto a marked empty patch of wooden planks with no lettering. Once the sack bears its own weight, the cable slackens. End with the cargo on the dock and the boat visibly lighter.

Audio: tiny wooden creaks, restrained pulley clicks and soft sack contact. No human voices, music, extra boats, spilled water, transforming sack, text or cuts.
```

## 06｜黑胶唱片城市：有轨电车追着唱针转弯

```text
Create a 10-second, 16:9 miniature city animation built along one groove of a stationary black vinyl record. One cream-and-green tram stands on curved rails between tiny brick buildings. A full-size turntable tonearm is parked above the record's outer edge, clearly separate from the miniature track. The record itself never spins.

[0-3s] Start a slow camera arc that follows the rail curve. The tram closes its doors and begins moving. Warm windows glow in two nearby buildings; keep all structures fixed to the record.
[3-7s] The tram rounds the curve under a small footbridge. Its roof clears the bridge physically and its wheels remain on the rails. The full-size stylus stays safely above and outside the tram path, providing scale contrast.
[7-10s] The tram stops beside an empty platform with a blank sign. One door opens to reveal an empty warm interior. Ease the camera to a stop with the stylus and tram both readable.

Audio: a small electric motor, gentle rail squeal and one door chime. No recorded song, passengers, spinning buildings, collisions, labels, logos or cuts.
```

## 07｜雪靴里的滑雪场：缆车穿过鞋带山谷

<!-- catalog:copy:start -->
[复制全文 / Download TXT](copy/miniature-worlds-07.txt) · [本页索引 / Case index](#case-index)
<!-- catalog:copy:end -->

```text
Create a 10-second, 16:9 winter miniature scene inside one upright dark-blue snow boot. White artificial snow forms a tiny slope from its open cuff down toward the toe. One red cable-car cabin hangs on a taut cable stretched between two visible miniature towers attached to the boot. Thick shoelaces form fixed valley ridges.

[0-3s] Make a slow camera push while keeping the boot sole and cuff visible. The red cabin begins moving uphill. A small pile of powder falls from the lower tower roof and lands on the slope.
[3-7s] Follow the cabin as it passes between two lace ridges. Its hanger remains attached to the cable, and the cabin stays upright with one subtle sway. Keep the cable taut and both support towers stationary.
[7-10s] The cabin arrives at a small station near the cuff and stops. A warm light turns on inside the station. End with the complete boot outline still recognizable.

Audio: quiet cable-wheel clicks, faint winter wind and one small stop mechanism. No people, avalanche, music, extra cabins, growing snow, text or cuts.
```

## 08｜机械键盘夜市：空格键升起一座小食亭

<!-- catalog:copy:start -->
[复制全文 / Download TXT](copy/miniature-worlds-08.txt) · [本页索引 / Case index](#case-index)
<!-- catalog:copy:end -->

```text
Create a 10-second, 16:9 miniature night-market animation across a black mechanical keyboard with blank keycaps. A tiny folded metal food kiosk rests flat on the spacebar. Two unlit miniature lanterns hang from its roof frame. No people are present. Keep the surrounding keys full-sized and stationary.

[0-3s] Lower the camera toward keycap height while preserving the keyboard outline. The spacebar depresses once under the kiosk's weight and returns. This mechanical motion triggers the kiosk roof to rise on four visible telescoping posts.
[3-7s] Two side panels unfold along their hinges to form a counter and canopy. The lanterns switch on with warm light, revealing three miniature steamed buns already arranged on a tray. The buns do not materialize during the opening.
[7-10s] A narrow wisp of steam rises from the tray and dissipates above the roof. Stop the camera on the open kiosk, with neighbouring blank keys establishing its tiny scale.

Audio: one satisfying spacebar click, small hinge ticks and a restrained steam hiss. No music, voices, lettering, logos, added kiosks, moving neighbouring keys or cuts.
```
