## Adapted source

The original Flaq AI copyright and MIT notice are retained in [LICENSE](../LICENSE), with SeaImagine's adaptation notice added.

The seven collections retain all 60 numbered recipes and all original copyable control blocks. The prompt-design, multilingual, API, and reference-video guides derive from that source. Original scenario authorship belongs to the source project; this adaptation does not claim that inherited prompts or images were newly created here.

## Language coverage

The SeaImagine model-page language switcher lists English, Japanese, Portuguese, Spanish, German, Russian, French, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Korean, Thai, Vietnamese, Arabic, Indonesian, and Italian. Website paths use `cn` and `tw`; language standards use `zh-CN` and `zh-TW`. These are intentionally different.

Localized READMEs provide a browser quickstart, six complete illustrated examples, four locally explained video studies, restored source teaching notes, and collection links. Shared prompt control text remains English. The first five inherited collections have Chinese explanations; the two advanced collections have English explanations. This is not a claim that every supporting document is translated into all 15 languages.
